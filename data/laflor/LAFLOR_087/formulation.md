Choose one aircraft design, one charger and an integer battery mass from 50 to 300 kg. Usable energy (kWh) is 0.32 times battery mass. For each occupied or empty leg, energy is total aircraft mass times the sum of the cruise energy per kg-km times distance and hover power per kg times two minutes in hours. Propagate state of charge by equality across flights and charging events. Enforce the usable capacity immediately after each charge and nonnegative charge on landing. Bound each charging event by power times available ground time and check takeoff mass and battery power. Daily cost includes aircraft and battery purchase amortization, eight chargers, maintenance, purchased flight energy and cycle wear on daytime recharge as specified in the statement. The reference MILP linearizes only the product of battery mass and a binary design choice.

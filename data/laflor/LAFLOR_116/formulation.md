Enumerate subsets of four candidate ports and the ground/air choice for each of three markets. Add 500 per open port. Ground travel costs passenger volume times distance divided by 0.5. Air trips use ground access to and egress from the nearest open ports and air speed 2 between distinct open ports. Add 0.5 times the product of aggregate flows on the two crossing diagonals. Choose the configuration of least total cost. With a fixed port set, the crossing penalty is multilinear in the market shares and an optimum exists at a pure mode choice for each market.
